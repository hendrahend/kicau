# Code of Conduct

Kicau is a small project for people who like birds and open software. Be the
kind of person others want to bird-watch with.

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks — in issues, PRs, or chat.
- Respect that contributors have different skill levels and backgrounds.
- Field recordings shared in issues should be your own or openly licensed.

Maintainers may remove contributions or block participants who repeatedly
violate this. Reports: open an issue titled "CoC report" (or email the
maintainer listed in `SECURITY.md`).
